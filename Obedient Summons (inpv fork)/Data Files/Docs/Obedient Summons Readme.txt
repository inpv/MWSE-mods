============
Description
============
Allows you to give basic orders to your summoned creatures. 
Adds a new spell, called "Soul Release", allowing you to summon a soul trapped in a soul gem. You can now have Vivec or Almalexia as obedient summons!
-> To give orders to your summon, simply activate them like you would to talk to them.
-> To summon a soultrapped being, place the filled soulgem into the world and cast the "Soul Release" spell, targetting the soulgem.
